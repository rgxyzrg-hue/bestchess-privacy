BEST CHESS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy describes how the BEST CHESS app for Android ("the app") handles information. It is written from the app's actual behaviour.

Publisher / contact: BEST CHESS — rgxyzrg@gmail.com

Summary: BEST CHESS works entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app does not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or calendar access.
  • No device identifiers or advertising ID.
  • No usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics are sent anywhere.
  • The app has no internet access permission, so it cannot send data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps these on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • your settings (sound, theme, board and piece style);
  • your saved games (moves, result, date, and any PGN you chose to review);
  • your puzzle progress (puzzle rating, XP, streak, recently played puzzle numbers).

This information never leaves your device through the app. You can delete single saved games inside the app, or everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Android may include the app's private data in your own device backup to your Google account, if you have backup turned on in your phone settings. That backup is managed by Android and Google under your account and is not accessible to us.

Files and clipboard

  • If you open a PGN file, the app reads only the file you pick in Android's file picker, on your device.
  • If you tap "Paste", the app reads the text currently in your clipboard to fill the box. Nothing is stored or sent elsewhere.

Third-party components

The app includes open-source and other third-party components, including the Stockfish chess engine (which runs as a separate program on your device), fonts, chess piece artwork, and public-domain puzzle and opening data. These components are used locally by the app and do not send your information anywhere. Their applicable licenses are listed in the app under Settings → Open source licenses.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app's behaviour changes (for example, an online feature is added), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
